Hosts own accepted state, time, saved records and physics/render integration.
ManualWindState uses meteorological FROM degrees and knots. No JSBSim unit
conversion, flight implementation, independent tick loop or weather provider.
Advanced profile/cloud/thermodynamic/turbulence/live weather stages remain absent.
Pure root imports without React, DOM or renderer initialization. Optional React
controls receive host state/actions; retain source compatibility honestly.
Use only immutable reviewed source and preserve original licenses/notices/history.
Reserve checks through the coordinator, capped at 50% workers; owner .tmp/.cache
caches and retained dated build logs. No servers, benchmarks or native/WASM builds.
Pinned shared foss-earth/ci owns attribution/conformance. Metadata checks include
docs-only changes. Never add AI author, committer or coauthor attribution.

You cannot have AI as a co-author, or your contributions will be rejected.
